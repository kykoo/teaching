Tamar Bridge: Key Specifications

  • Opened to traffic in 1961, located between Saltash (Cornwall) and Plymouth (Devon), UK.
  • Total structural length: about 642 m, and it was the longest suspension bridge in the UK when opened.
  • Main span: 335 m (with side + approach spans making up the full 642 m).
  • Major upgrade (1998-2001): bridge was strengthened and widened from 3 to 5 lanes while kept open to traffic, with a dedicated pedestrian/cycle lane.
