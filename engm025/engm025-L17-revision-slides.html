Nyquist Frequency

For a signal sampled at a sampling frequency \(f_s\), the Nyquist frequency is defiend as

\[ f_N = \frac{f_s}{2} \]

  • \(f_N\) is the highest frequency that can be uniquely represented
  • Frequencies above \(f_N\) will alias into lower frequencies
  • Each sinusoid needs at least two samples per cycle

Power Spectral Density

\[ P_{YY}(f) = \lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{1}{T}\,Y(f)Y(f)^* = \lim_{T\to\infty} \frac{1}{T}\,|Y(f)|^2 \]
